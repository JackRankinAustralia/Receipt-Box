ReceiptGo Support

Need help with ReceiptGo?

ReceiptGo is designed to make capturing and organising receipts simple. If something is not working as expected, or you have a question about your account, receipts, automatic scanning or reports, contact us.

Email support@receiptgo.com.au

When contacting support

Describe the problem and, where useful, tell us what device you are using. Do not send passwords, recovery codes, API keys or other account credentials.

Password problems

If you have forgotten your password, use “Forgot password?” on the ReceiptGo sign-in screen. ReceiptGo will send a secure password-reset link.

Account deletion

You can delete your account inside ReceiptGo. If you no longer have access to the app, you can still request account deletion without reinstalling ReceiptGo.

Policies
