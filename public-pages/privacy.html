ReceiptGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

ReceiptGo is provided by National Events Pty Ltd (ABN 74 687 975 337, ACN 687 975 337), an Australian company.

ReceiptGo is a receipt capture, storage and expense-recording application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through ReceiptGo, how and why we use it, the services we use to process it, and the choices available to users.

Information you provide

  • Your email address and account information.
  • Photographs, images and PDF copies of receipts.
  • Information extracted from receipts, including supplier, date, total, GST, category and other receipt details.
  • Information you manually enter or edit.
  • Communications you send to support.

ReceiptGo accesses your camera or photo library only when you choose to photograph or select a receipt, subject to your device permissions.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and maintain ReceiptGo accounts.
  • Securely store and retrieve receipts.
  • Automatically read receipt information.
  • Organise, search and review receipt records.
  • Generate reports and exports.
  • Provide account and password recovery.
  • Maintain service security and reliability.
  • Respond to support requests.

ReceiptGo does not sell personal information.

Automatic receipt scanning

Automatic scanning may transmit receipt images and associated data required for processing to third-party artificial intelligence services. ReceiptGo uses the Google Gemini API to automatically read information from receipt images. Receipt images and related data required for this process are transmitted to Google for automated processing.

Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication, database services and private receipt-file storage.
  • Google Gemini — automatic extraction of receipt information.
  • Resend — transactional email and receipt of support email.
  • Render — hosting of ReceiptGo web services.

Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including private receipt storage and authorised access controls. Information may be processed or stored outside Australia. Third-party providers operate their own infrastructure and privacy practices. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Receipt information is retained while your account remains active unless you delete individual records earlier. You can delete individual records or delete your ReceiptGo account from within the app. Account deletion removes the account and associated ReceiptGo data, subject to information that must legitimately be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory reasons.

You can also make an external deletion request using the Delete Account page.

Your choices

  • View and edit receipt information.
  • Delete individual receipts.
  • Control camera and photo-library permissions.
  • Export receipt information.
  • Delete your account.
  • Contact support.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as ReceiptGo or applicable requirements change. The current version and its effective date will remain published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@receiptgo.com.au.
